**028**: A human MUST merge every pull request, always — never automation, regardless of how trivial the change looks or how green CI is.

Rationale: see `engineering-practices.md`'s "CI, deploys, and verification" section — a passing build is not proof the app works, and merge authority is a human judgment call the tooling shouldn't be trusted to make on its own.

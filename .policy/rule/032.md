**032**: Content that is durable but personal/environment-specific (a local path, a personal tool preference, a machine-specific workaround) MUST go in `CLAUDE.local.md` (gitignored), never a tracked file.

Rationale: Keeps personal, environment-specific notes out of a repo that strangers read, while still persisting them locally in a gitignored file..
